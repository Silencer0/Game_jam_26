# New relations between past , present, future enemies
- follows regular time rules
- Three frames of reference exist: past present and future. Enemies that exist in the past without dying exist in the present and future frames as well. 
- When an enemy is killed in the past it's future representations also vanish/die. If an enemy is killed in the future, the enemy does not vanihs in the past frames.
- There can be some enemies who spawn directly into the present or future. If an enemy is spawned in a past frame he has to be present in every future frame.

# Switching panels new mechanic
- A player can switch positions of panels between past present and future, in the escape menu. 
- Now we reproces the entire enemy state considering this to be the new true order of events, any contradictions will kill the enemies which were subject to it. For example: We have an enemy-'A', in the past frame and the present, We now kill the enemy in the present and switch the panels such that the present is now the new past. Now there is a contradiction where A is in the present but not the past (this rule only applies for enemies who were subjected to the panel switching).
- We show that the same enemy is present in 2 or 3 panels in the escape menu by showing a string connecting them between the panels. If that enemey is dead in a panel we reduce their opacity a bit and put an X on it. So the player knows the enemy is dead in this panel so the player can accurately and strategically switch the panels around to kill the enemies through time contradiction.
- Doing this also spawns new enemies according to the time laws. If an enemy was switched to the past , then he should now have instances in all future frames. 

# Scoring , Mults
- all regular kills add +1 to the addition bar. All kills made through panel switching add +1 to the Multiplier bar. The damage the player deals with his attacks is dependant on the add bar value x the multiplier bar value. 
- Using the Time dilation ultimate reduces the Mult value. 

# Enemy spawning
- All enemies that ever spawn in the game always spawn in the future, And slowly trickle down in the present past depending on the gap between the panels. 

# Time dilation:
- The player has the ability to move the frames closer or farther from each other by either going slower than the speed of light or faster.
- If i am in the past an i want to go closer in time to the present i would dilate slower than the speed of light.
- The ability to dilate is an ult that the player gets when he presses E. He needs to get a certain score to use this ability (add a bar which shows the remquired amount, this is the Mult bar).
- When the ult is used he can hold E for however much time he wants until the point meter runs out. The closer two frames get the more light points are required to cover the gap. The player can only activate the dilation ult whne the bar is full but can dilate as much as he requires and can stop anytime before the bar depletes. It also slows down the time in that frame for everythinng except the player.
- Main goal: achieve singularity by overlapping all frames onto each other so there is only one final singular frame.

# Final Goal of the game 
- To get all panels to come together by using the time dilation mechanic to reduce the time difference between the panels. Time dilation in the past brings the time difference between the past and the presnet closer. Time dilation in the future brings the time dilation between the future and present closer. So everytime the player uses the ult ability they get closer to finishing the game.
